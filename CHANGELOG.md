# Changelog

## 0.4 - First release

### Added
- **Minigames Forever**: a collection of simple minigames in one window. Game list on the left, game on the right. Open it with `/mgf`.
- **Bejeweled** (`/bj`): match three or more gems, chain reactions with combo points, animated swapping, falling and bursting gems with sparkles, automatic reshuffle when no moves are left, saved high score.
- **Blackjack** (`/bjk`, `/blackjack`, `/21`): Hit, Stand and Double, 4-deck shoe, Blackjack pays 3:2, dealer stands on 17, in-game chips (no real gold), hand-drawn cards with suit symbols, animated dealing and card flips, saved chip count.
- **2048** (`/2048`): arrow keys, WASD, mouse swipe or on-screen buttons, sliding and merging animations, saved high score and automatic save of the running game.
- **Settings** (gear icon): language, animation speed (slow / normal / fast), effects on/off, gem style (icons or plain colours).
- **Languages:** English, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português. Defaults to the client language.

### Known limitations
- Blackjack has no splitting and no insurance.
- Bejeweled has no timed mode or special gems.
